Work with us

SysManage manages Windows, Linux, macOS and the BSDs from one control plane. If your customers run a mixed estate, that is a problem you are probably already being paid to solve the hard way.

Who we are looking to work with

Managed service providers

You manage fleets you did not choose, across operating systems you would not have picked. One control plane means one operational model per customer instead of one per platform, and per-tenant database isolation means one deployment can serve many customers without their data sharing a table.

Resellers and distributors

Professional+ and Enterprise are licensed tiers with a straightforward commercial model. If you already sell infrastructure tooling into regulated, air-gapped or BSD-heavy environments, this fills a gap most vendors ignore.

Systems integrators

The core is AGPLv3 and the API is documented, so an integration is not a black box. Air-gapped deployment, federation and enrollment automation are first-class rather than bolted on.

OEM and embedded

If you ship appliances or devices that a customer then has to manage, an agent that runs on every platform you support may be cheaper than building the management layer yourself.

Where we actually are

It is worth being straight about this rather than letting you discover it after a call.

  • Ready today: the product works, ships native packages for every supported platform, and is in production use. Multi-tenancy, air-gapped deployment and federation are built and documented.
  • We are small: SysManage is early-stage. You will deal directly with the person who wrote the code, which cuts both ways — fast answers, but no 24/7 partner desk yet.
  • No formal partner programme yet: there is no tiered portal, no MDF, no certification track. If those are prerequisites for you, we are too early. If a direct commercial conversation works, we are ready now.
