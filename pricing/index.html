If a license lapses, your data is still yours

Restoring backups -- encrypted ones included -- and exporting your backup keys keep working without a license. What stops is new paid capability, such as creating new keys or scheduled verification. A license check never locks you out of your own systems.

Works offline and air-gapped

Licensed capabilities run without a connection to us. Air-gapped installations are supported in Enterprise, with content brought in through a signed media workflow.
