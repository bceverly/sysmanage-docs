Isolation that actually isolates

Most multi-tenant tools store every client's hosts in one shared table and filter by a tenant column. One missed WHERE clause and a client sees another client's data. SysManage takes a different approach: each tenant gets a dedicated database. Isolation is enforced by the architecture, not by remembering to filter every query.

🗄️

Dedicated database per tenant

Every client fleet lives in its own database — not a shared table with a tenant column. Cross-tenant leakage isn't a bug you have to guard against; it's structurally impossible.

🔐

Per-tenant brokered credentials

Each tenant's secrets are brokered and sealed independently through OpenBAO, so one client's credentials are never reachable from another client's context.

📍

Per-tenant data residency & deletion

Because each client's data is its own database, offboarding is clean: drop the tenant database and the client's data is gone — provable residency and deletion, no orphaned rows.

⚡

Self-service tenant provisioning

Stand up a new client tenant — database, vault, and all — without hand-building infrastructure. Onboarding a client is a workflow, not a project.

🖥️

One operator console across all tenants

Your technicians work from a single pane of glass and switch between client fleets in a click — without spinning up a separate deployment for each account.

🧩

Consistent management on every OS

Whatever mix each client runs — Windows, Linux, macOS, FreeBSD, OpenBSD, or NetBSD — your team manages it the same way, with the same workflows.

Every client fleet, one operator view

Switch between tenants from a single operator console instead of juggling a separate login for every account. Inventory, patch status, compliance, and vulnerability posture are all a click away, per client, without ever leaving the platform your team already knows.

  • Switch client context in one click — no re-login, no separate portals
  • Consistent inventory, patching, and compliance workflows across every tenant
  • Onboard and offboard clients without touching another tenant's data

Multi-site and federation

Clients rarely live in one location. SysManage manages distributed and federated sites, so a single client with offices, data centers, or remote branches stays coherent under one tenant — and your practice keeps its footing as accounts grow across geographies.

  • Manage geographically distributed sites under one tenant
  • Federate multiple sites for large or multi-location clients
  • Scale a client account without fracturing it into disconnected deployments

Heterogeneous by default

MSP clients are never uniform. One account is Windows-heavy with a fleet of laptops; the next runs Linux servers and FreeBSD appliances; another mixes macOS, OpenBSD, and NetBSD. SysManage manages them all the same way — and managing anything a client throws at you, consistently, is the whole point of an MSP tool. The core is AGPLv3, and the interface ships in 14 languages.
