The hidden cost of one tool per operating system

The per-platform tool is rarely the expensive part. The real cost lives in the seams between them: duplicated licensing, duplicated training, duplicated deployments, and the coordination tax of stitching six consoles together every time something needs to change. Consolidation reclaims all of it.

💰

🔑

One inventory for the whole fleet

SysManage keeps a single, live inventory of every managed host regardless of what it runs. Windows, every major Linux distribution, macOS, and FreeBSD, OpenBSD, and NetBSD are all first-class citizens — not afterthoughts bolted onto a Windows-first product. Real-time WebSocket monitoring means the state you see is the state that exists right now.

  • Windows, every major Linux distribution, macOS, and FreeBSD, OpenBSD, and NetBSD as equals
  • Real-time host state over persistent WebSocket connections, not stale nightly scans
  • Unified reporting that reads across the OS boundary instead of stopping at it
