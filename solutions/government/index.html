SysManage was designed around these constraints, not adapted to them after the fact.

It helps you demonstrate where you stand against the standards you're held to, rather than leaving you to assemble spreadsheets by hand.

The platforms defense actually runs

A great deal of defense, networking, and critical infrastructure runs on the BSDs — and most management tools treat them as an afterthought, if they support them at all. SysManage gives FreeBSD, OpenBSD, and NetBSD the same first-class treatment as every other platform: full inventory, patching, compliance, and vulnerability management, on one console, alongside Windows, Linux, and macOS.

  • FreeBSD, OpenBSD, and NetBSD managed as first-class citizens
  • One console for all six operating systems — no gaps to paper over
  • Full patching, compliance, and vulnerability coverage on every platform

Auditable by design

Because the core is open source under AGPLv3, security teams and accreditors can read the code rather than trust a vendor's word for what it does. Combined with on-premises and air-gapped deployment — where nothing leaves your network and nothing phones home — that adds up to a materially stronger trust posture than a closed, cloud-hosted alternative can offer.
